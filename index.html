FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers on how IsBar operates.

Does IsBar ever damage or modify my original sequence?

Never. IsBar is built with a strict non-destructive architecture. When you execute Auto Cut, IsBar duplicates your active sequence and performs all cut operations on the duplicate copy. Your original cut, markers, and audio tracks remain 100% untouched.

What is Smart Punch-In and how does it work?

In talking-head videos, constant single-angle shots feel flat. Smart Punch-In automatically applies subtle, alternating zoom scales (such as 108% or 110%) to dialogue segments between cuts. This creates natural camera variation like a multi-cam shoot without manual keyframing.

How does the Floating FX Console work in Windows?

The FX Console is a dedicated floating search popup controlled by a global hotkey (e.g. Ctrl + `). Type the name of any installed effect to apply it instantly, use Ctrl + 1...9 quick slots for your most-used plugins, capture Program Monitor frames, or sample exact HEX colors using the built-in eyedropper.

How does the Waveform Drag & Drop feature work?

In the Audio library tab, you can view the audio waveform of any sound effect or music track. Use your mouse to draw a selection box around the exact section you want (or use bracket keys), then simply click and drag that box directly onto Video/Audio Track 2 in your Premiere Pro timeline.

What is the AI B-Roll Assembly & MCP Bridge?

IsBar can take an SRT subtitle file, run an AI visual planning model (via Ollama, LM Studio, or OpenRouter), find matching stock video from Pexels or FlexClip, and automatically assemble relevant B-roll on an empty video track. The MCP bridge allows external AI clients (like Claude Code or Cursor) to inspect context and trigger assembly.

Do I need to pay a recurring monthly subscription?

No! IsBar is sold as a perpetual license. You pay once and own that version forever with lifetime minor updates. There are no surprise monthly billing charges.
