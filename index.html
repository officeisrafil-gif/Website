FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about IsBar.

Does IsBar ever damage or modify my original sequence?

Never. IsBar is built with a strict non-destructive architecture. When you execute Auto Cut, IsBar duplicates your active sequence and performs all cut operations on the duplicate copy. Your original cut, markers, and audio tracks remain 100% untouched.

How does the Waveform Drag & Drop feature work?

In the Sound Design tab, you can view the audio waveform of any sound effect or music track. Use your mouse to draw a selection box around the exact section you want (or use bracket keys), then simply click and drag that box directly onto Video/Audio Track 2 in your Premiere Pro timeline.

Which versions of Premiere Pro are supported?

IsBar supports Adobe Premiere Pro 2024, 2025, and the latest Premiere Pro 2026. It works seamlessly on both Windows 10/11 and macOS (including Intel and Apple Silicon M1, M2, M3, and M4).

Do I need to pay a recurring monthly subscription?

No! IsBar is sold as a perpetual license. You pay once and own that version forever with lifetime minor updates. There are no surprise monthly billing charges.

Do I need to manually export a WAV file before using Auto Cut?

No. In Premiere Pro 2025/2026, IsBar automatically exports and processes the active sequence audio in the background from 00:00. If you are on an older build or want to use a master audio track, a WAV fallback option is also available.

How do I install the extension after purchase?

Installation takes less than 60 seconds. You receive an automated installer for Windows (.exe) and macOS (.pkg) or a direct drag-and-drop CEP folder. Once installed, simply open Premiere Pro and find IsBar under Window > Extensions > IsBar.
